## How the terms were made

The English sense notes and example sentences, and the Russian and Serbian terms, glosses and
grammar notes, were generated and checked with large language models: DeepSeek
(deepseek-v4-flash), Qwen (Qwen3.6, run locally) and Claude (Anthropic's Claude Sonnet). Each
Russian and Serbian term was translated independently by two models, checked by a blind
back-translation and automatic rules, and disputes were adjudicated by a third model, which also
audited a random sample. **No human has reviewed the terms**, except the 300 entries carried
over from the earlier reviewed starter list. Mistakes are possible; the review reports in
`reports/` show how each term was decided and which terms were held back (quarantined).
