# Serbian, A1

800 meanings: 760 ok, 37 collision, 1 quarantined, 2 duplicate synonyms in Serbian.
Translated 500 (v1 kept 300); A and B agreed on 423/500 (85%); accepted without adjudication 396/500 (79%); adjudicated 99 (a 59, b 27, other 12, unsure 1); level overrides 0.

Conventions: nouns in the nominative singular, adjectives masculine singular, Russian and Serbian verbs as the imperfective infinitive with the perfective partner in the note (`pf:`), Serbian Ekavian in Cyrillic, Russian with ё, English in British spelling. Notes: `g` gender (only where the ending doesn't show it; `pl` = plural-only), `pl` irregular plural, `pf` perfective partner, `past` English irregular forms. Hints are the sense notes shown with a translation where several meanings share a term. Statuses: `ok` (offered to learners of the language), `collision` (another meaning has the same word in this language: shown to its native speakers, not offered to its learners), `quarantined` (failed verification: never shown), counted apart when it is a `duplicate synonym` (another meaning has the same term in this language and no hint tells them apart: the other one keeps it).
